# Video 2 — Marketer / Content Creator: One proof, eight pieces of content

**Target length:** ~85 seconds · **Audience:** in-house marketers, content managers, founders who do their own marketing.

**Promise of the video:** By the end, the viewer knows that one approved testimonial in NotiProof becomes a week of multi-channel content — automatically and on-brand.

---

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00 – 0:10)

| VO | On-screen | Cues |
|----|-----------|------|
| "Every approved testimonial in NotiProof is a week of marketing content waiting to happen. Tweet, LinkedIn post, email subject line, ad copy, website quote — all written for you, in your brand voice." | Six small content cards burst out from a single glowing proof card in the centre — each card labelled with a content type (Twitter, LinkedIn, Email, Ad Headline, Website Quote, Short Caption). | Stagger the burst with a snappy spring. |
