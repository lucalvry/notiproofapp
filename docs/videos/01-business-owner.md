# Video 1 — Business Owner: From signup to live notifications

**Target length:** ~75 seconds · **Audience:** Shopify / WooCommerce store owners, SaaS founders, anyone running a website who wants social proof.

**Promise of the video:** By the end, the viewer knows the exact 4 steps to go from "no account" to "real notifications showing on my live site."

| VO | On-screen | Cues |
|----|-----------|------|
| "Every day, real people buy from you, sign up, or leave reviews. Fade in fast, hold 3s. |
