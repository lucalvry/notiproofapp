# Video 4 — Customer: Share your testimonial in 60 seconds

**Target length:** ~60 seconds · **Audience:** the end customer who receives a testimonial request email. This video is meant to be sent *to* a customer (or embedded in the request email) so they know exactly what to expect.

**Promise of the video:** By the end, the viewer knows submitting a testimonial takes under a minute and is dead simple.

---

## Scene 1 — Hook: the email (0:00 – 0:10)

| VO | On-screen | Cues |
|----|-----------|------|
| "Someone you've bought from just asked for a quick testimonial. Here's exactly what's going to happen — it takes about a minute." | Stylised email inbox. One email highlighted: from "Acme Co.", subject: **"We'd love to hear about your experience"**. Preview line: *"Hi Sarah, thanks for choosing us! Could you take 60 seconds to share a quick testimonial?"* | Cursor clicks the email open. Inside, a single big button **"Share your experience"**. |
